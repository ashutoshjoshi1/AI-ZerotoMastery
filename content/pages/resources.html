A short, opinionated list. Each item earns its place; you do not need all of them. Start with the one that matches the part of the course you found hardest. Model names, prices and library APIs change quickly, so for anything product-specific prefer the official documentation over books. Part numbers are the ten parts of the course; part IX, the wider field, is where most of the newer entries sit.

Datasets for practice

  • scikit-learn built-ins (breast cancer, digits, diabetes) and OpenML for classical ML.
  • Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 for image classification; Oxford-IIIT Pet and COCO for segmentation and detection.
  • Tiny Shakespeare and Project Gutenberg texts for language modelling.
  • Hugging Face Datasets hub for almost everything else, including instruction, preference and reasoning datasets.
  • Gymnasium environments (CartPole, LunarLander, the Atari games) for reinforcement learning.
  • MovieLens for recommenders, and Cora and the other datasets that ship with PyTorch Geometric for graphs.
  • Public environmental data (air-quality networks, weather stations, satellite products) and the M5 forecasting competition data for time series, forecasting and anomaly-detection capstones.
