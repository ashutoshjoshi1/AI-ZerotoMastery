The full lesson, with code, hands-on exercises and a notebook, is on its way. Here is what it will cover, so you know where it fits in the course.

  • KV cache memory maths, and how grouped-query attention changes it
  • Continuous batching, PagedAttention and prefix caching
  • Quantisation in depth: int8 and int4, group-wise scales, GPTQ and AWQ, GGUF
  • Speculative decoding, sampling and constrained decoding; choosing an inference engine
