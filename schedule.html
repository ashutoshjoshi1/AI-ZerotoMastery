Daily schedule

The whole course as 111 one-hour sessions. At five sessions a week that is about 23 weeks; at seven a week, about 16 weeks. These checkboxes are the same ones you see in each lesson, so ticking either place records the same progress.

Some sessions will run long, especially the hands-on ones from the deep learning lessons onwards. Let them. Splitting a session over two days is better than skipping the exercise.

The lessons in part IX, the wider field, stand on their own. Take them in order, pick the ones your work needs, or interleave them with the parts before.
