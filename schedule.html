Daily schedule

The whole course as 80 one-hour sessions. At five sessions a week that is about 16 weeks; at seven a week, about 12 weeks. The checkboxes here are the same ones you see at the top of each lesson, so ticking either place records the same progress.

Some sessions will run long, especially the hands-on ones in parts IV to VI. Let them. Splitting a session over two days is better than skipping the exercise.
